2023-Present Lead Graduate Student Researcher, Development of Integrated Seismic Risk, Recovery, and Risk-Sharing Frameworks for Community Resilience.

Evaluated spatial and temporal trends and socioeconomic drivers of seismic retrofit activity in Los Angeles; quantified retrofit-induced seismic risk-reduction benefits; assessed the effects of retrofitting on post-earthquake functional recovery of single-family homes; and developed stochastic reciprocal exchange models to investigate equitable community-based seismic risk sharing.
