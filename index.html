01 / RESEARCH

Research at
the center.

Structural & Earthquake Engineering.

I develop integrated seismic risk, recovery, and risk-sharing frameworks for community resilience. My work examines retrofit activity in Los Angeles, the benefits of retrofitting, post-earthquake recovery, and reciprocal exchanges.

RESEARCH AREA 01 / 04 · OBSERVE

Retrofit trends and socioeconomic drivers

Evaluating where and when seismic retrofits occur in Los Angeles, and the socioeconomic drivers of adoption.

RESEARCH AREA 02 / 04 · STRENGTHEN

Risk reduction from seismic retrofits

Quantifying the seismic risk-reduction benefits of retrofitting, with a focus on cripple wall retrofits in Los Angeles.

RESEARCH AREA 03 / 04 · RECOVER

Post-earthquake functional recovery

Assessing how retrofitting affects the return to function of single-family homes after an earthquake.

Community-based reciprocal risk sharing

Developing stochastic reciprocal exchange models to investigate equitable community-based seismic risk sharing.

04 / ABOUT

Engineering with
curiosity & purpose.

Connecting earthquake engineering with community resilience.

I am Godfred Opoku Ababio, a Ph.D. candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, specializing in Structural & Earthquake Engineering.

Originally from Ghana, I earned my bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering at KNUST before coming to the United States for graduate studies at UCLA, where I am now a Ph.D. candidate in Structural & Earthquake Engineering.

My research spans data-driven performance-based engineering, seismic hazard and risk assessment, building functional recovery, and community-based risk pooling.
